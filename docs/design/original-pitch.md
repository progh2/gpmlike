# Original pitch — Nuri Term / 누리학기

Working title for *our* game. **No Gunparade March names.**

## Logline

**KO:** 항구 방위학원의 하루를 살아 가는 오리지널 웹 게임. NPC는 제 발로 움직이고, 메카는 거대 로봇이 아니라 정비 가능한 쇼어프레임이다.

**EN:** A coastal defense academy sim in the browser. NPCs keep their own errands. Combat is mecha-lite: compact **Shoreframes**, not a licensed war machine.

## Setting

**Nuri Bay** (누리만) is a mid-sized harbor city after the **Rift Wake** — a decades-long fault in offshore space-weather, not an alternate-1945 alien invasion. Things that should stay under the seam sometimes walk inland. People call them **Seamkin** (틈새). They are fauna and weather more than an empire.

**Nuri Bay Defense Academy** (누리만 방위학원) trains cadets who still take exams. Days are classes, workshop shifts, and **Third Watch** (3번 당직) rotations on the sea wall. The academy is a school that happens to own walkers.

## Mecha-lite

**Shoreframes** (쇼어프레임) are two-to-four-meter service walkers: salt-proof joints, swap-out arms, ugly paint. They are closer to harbor cranes with legs than to celebrity robots. A cadet who cannot repair a seal should not brag about a sortie.

Roles on Third Watch (original names only):

| Role | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Frame op | Pilots a Shoreframe |
| Wall scout | Foot team with sensors |
| Watch lead | Calls intents, not letter codes |
| Tender | Repair / ammo between periods |
| Comms | Roof and clinic relays |

## Living NPCs

Every named cadet has a **schedule graph** (issue #8). They get hungry, skip optional drills, and form opinions of each other. The player is one node, not the sun.
