| Axis · 축 | Game (PS, 2000) · 게임 | Anime (2003, 12 ep) · 애니 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 1. Structure & goals · 구조와 목표

### EN
- **Game:** Survive a dated window while *any* lifestyle is valid if you live — ace, mechanic, recluse, matchmaker, etc. Systems generate parallel stories.
- **Anime:** Condensed war + school drama with a clear protagonist–heroine relationship arc; mid-to-late episodes lean romantic comedy relative to the game’s sandbox bleakness.

### KO
- **게임:** 기간 내 생존이 목표이며, 그 안에서 역할·생활은 자유. 시스템이 병렬 서사를 생성.
- **애니:** 전쟁·학원 드라마로 압축, 주인공–히로인 관계축이 명확. 중후반은 게임의 샌드박스 암울함보다 청춘·연애 톤이 강해짐(위키·리뷰 합의).

---

## 2. Lore & world framing · 설정·세계관 프레이밍

### EN
- Game carries heavy “Nameless World / Seven Worlds” adjacent mysteries, cloning/generation tech, opaque megacorps — much of it optional / discoverable.
- Anime **deletes or replaces** most of that deep lore; introduces or emphasizes anime-facing war tools (e.g. decisive weapon framing abbreviated as PBE in public wiki) and a simpler corporate-military backdrop.
- Shared surface: student soldiers, Kumamoto school base, mecha vs phantom beasts — but **not** a 1:1 lore port.

### KO
- 게임은 깊은 이면설정(세대·클로닝·군벌 등)을 탐색 가능하게 둠.
- 애니는 대부분 **삭제·치환**, 시청용 전쟁도구·기업 배경으로 단순화.
- 표면만 공유(학병·학교 주둔·메카 vs 환수) — **설정 이식본이 아님**.

---

## 3. Characters & roles (systems lens) · 캐릭터·역할 (시스템 관점)

### EN
- **Game:** ~22 platoon members as full AI agents with jobs, skills, gazes, petitions; teachers/cat as systemic NPCs; NG+ unlocks more PCs.
- **Anime:** Roster trimmed/remapped (pilots vs ops vs scout labels change for several names); some game-important characters omitted from TV; drama CD / extras fill gaps differently.
- **Design lesson:** Anime proves a *linear slice* of the cast works for story; game proves *many agents + jobs* create replay. Original web game should pick one axis deliberately — or hybrid lightly.

### KO
- **게임:** 소대 전원이 직업·스킬·시선·진정을 가진 AI 에이전트.
- **애니:** 인원·역할 재배치, 일부 중요 인물 미등장.
- **설계 교훈:** 애니는 “캐스트 슬라이스+선형 서사”, 게임은 “다수 에이전트+직업”. 웹 오리지널은 축을 의식적으로 선택.

*No character dialogue or route spoilers collected.*

---

## 4. Combat & mecha presentation · 전투·메카 표현

### EN
- **Game:** Hexless tactics, Action Codes, weight/steps, loadout discipline, permanent loss, warfront feedback.
- **Anime:** Spectacle and character moments; mechs (HWT branding) portrayed as more widely deployed; formation size differs from game’s scarce specialist framing.
- Do **not** treat anime battle choreography as a mechanics bible.

### KO
- **게임:** 전술·코드·중량·영구손실·전황 피드백이 본체.
- **애니:** 연출·캐릭터 드라마 중심; 메카 보급·편성 규모가 게임과 다름.
- 애니 전투 연출을 메커닉 명세로 쓰지 말 것.

---

## 5. What each medium is “for” · 각 매체의 강점

| Medium | Best at · 잘하는 것 | Weak at · 약한 것 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game | Emergent peer society + systemic war stress | Delivering one polished “canonical” romance plot |
| Anime | Emotional clarity, music, fixed arcs in 12 eps | Capturing Karel-style NPC autonomy / soft-currency politics |

## 6. Sequel note (context only) · 후속작 메모

- **Gunparade Orchestra** (PS2 + later anime): same franchise family, different cast/regions; game design shifted more event-driven / different battle pacing (secondary essays). Useful as “what happens when you dilute Karel sandbox” — not as a systems clone target.
