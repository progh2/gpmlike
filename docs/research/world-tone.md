# world-tone.md — Tone & themes (systems-facing)
# 톤·테마 (시스템 설계용 / 고유명사 최소화)

**Rule:** Describe *feel* and *pressure*, not plot or cast names.  
**규칙:** 플롯·캐릭명 없이 *분위기·압력*만.

**Sources:** JP Wikipedia (game), Kimimi review (2022-03-14), anime wiki summary.

## 1. Core tension · 핵심 긴장

| EN | KO |
| --- | --- |
| Student life and soldier duty share one body and one calendar | 학생 일과 병사 의무가 **한 몸·한 달력**을 공유 |
| Survival to a seasonal pause beats “defeat the final boss” | 최종보스 격파보다 **시즌 휴지기까지 생존** |
| Normality is precious *because* the war is always nearby | 전쟁이 가까울수록 **일상의 소중함**이 커짐 |
| Player is a peer in a living platoon, not the sun of the cast | 플레이어는 주연 태양이 아니라 **동료 중 하나** |

## 2. Dual register · 이중 레지스터

### EN
- **Quiet register:** Classes, lunch, training spots, chatter, mood of a room, who looks at whom.
- **Loud register:** Sirens mid-schedule, loadout you forgot to prep, permanent loss of a peer, warfront pressure creeping.
- Design goal: the quiet register must stay *playable and tempting* even when the loud register is lethal — not pure misery sim, not pure school comedy.

### KO
- **조용한 층:** 수업·점심·훈련 장소·잡담·장 분위기·시선.
- **시끄러운 층:** 일과 중 사이렌, 미비한 출격 준비, 동료 영구 손실, 전황 악화.
- 목표: 치명적 층이 있어도 일상 층이 **하고 싶을 만큼** 남아 있어야 함. 순수 암울도, 순수 학원 코미디도 아님.

## 3. Themes useful for *our* original · 오리지널에 쓸 테마

Borrow as **theme axes**, invent fiction:

1. **Maintenance of the self** — fatigue, decay of unused skills, rest vs grind.  
2. **Soft power** — talk/politics cost a spendable social currency.  
3. **Reciprocity** — one-sided affection is fragile; mutual trust unlocks verbs.  
4. **Role identity** — frontline / scout / desk / wrench change *what a day feels like*.  
5. **Interrupt fairness** — crises should feel earned by war state, not random cruelty only.
6. **Peer agency** — NPCs petition, slack, romance, and reassign without the player.

Do **not** borrow: Black Moon mythology dumps, specific clan politics, trademarked song/march stakes, named medals as plot keys.

---

## 4. Tone pitfalls · 톤 함정

| Avoid · 피할 것 | Prefer · 선호 |
| --- | --- |
| Glorifying underage soldiers as cool aesthetic | Age-up cast or abstract “cadet academy”; clear content rating |
| Anime mid-run romance-comedy as the *systems* bible | Use anime for *slice* pacing only; systems from game reviews/wiki |
| Copying grimdark for its own sake | Keep “treasure normality” as counterweight |
| Protagonist-centric VN flags only | Systemic peer sim (lite) first |

---

## 5. One-line pitch filter · 한 줄 필터

> If a feature doesn’t change either **today’s calendar choice** or **tomorrow’s pressure**, it’s probably flavor — ship later.

> 오늘 캘린더 선택이나 내일 압력에 안 닿으면 플레이버 — 나중.
