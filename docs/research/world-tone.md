# World tone — *our* game

Tone goals for **Nuri Term** (누리학기). This is not a summary of another franchise’s lore. Public “school + war + ordinary Tuesday” *feel* is the only inspiration; names, history, and jokes are original.

## Pitch in one breath

A coastal defense academy tries to keep **classes, repairs, and friendships** on the calendar while the sea-fault called the **Rift Wake** occasionally throws **Seamkin** onto the shore. Cadets are tired, competent enough, and still teenagers. Giant-robot spectacle is rare; **showing up** is the drama.

## Mood targets

- **Ordinary first, extraordinary second.** Homeroom, lunch, a leaking workshop roof. Then a watch siren.
- **Living campus.** NPCs have errands you did not assign. The world should not pause when the player opens a menu.
- **Competence without invincibility.** Shoreframes are tools. A good day is “everyone came back and the essay is late.”
- **Warm, not syrupy.** Banter and care, but no harem-route marketing voice.
- **Loss is expensive, not edgy.** If we later add lasting injury or absence, it should change schedules and empty chairs — not shock-value cutscenes.
- **Korean / bilingual texture.** Place names and UI can be Korean-first; English is a peer language, not a localization afterthought.

## Visual / audio tone (later)

- Overcast harbor light, painted concrete, salt on glass. Avoid “generic military anime HUD” chrome copied from any existing title.
- Mecha-lite silhouettes: compact, serviceable, a little ugly.
- VRM cadets in school-adjacent clothes, not licensed character lookalikes.

## What we are not aiming at

- A retelling of a 2000 PlayStation campaign or the 2003 TV cour.
- Sekai-kei “the world ends because of one couple” as the only theme.
- Power-fantasy ace-pilot wish fulfillment as the default win.
- Grimdark teen-soldier exploitation.

## Play fantasy

You are a **cadet who also has a job on the watch bill**. You can lean repair, comms, or frame ops. The fantasy is *keeping a small community intact*, not collecting endings.
